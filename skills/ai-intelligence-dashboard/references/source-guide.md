# Source guide for AI intelligence dashboards

## Scientific research / papers

Prefer primary or research-index sources:

- arXiv categories: `cs.AI`, `cs.CL`, `cs.LG`, `cs.CV`, `stat.ML`
- Papers with Code trending/latest
- Semantic Scholar
- OpenReview for conference submissions and workshop papers
- ACL Anthology, NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, ICCV, EMNLP, AAAI, KDD
- Nature, Science, PNAS, ACM, IEEE when relevant
- Lab/company research pages only when they link to a paper or technical report

Search patterns:

- `site:arxiv.org AI OR "large language model" after:<date>`
- `site:openreview.net "large language model" "2026"`
- `site:paperswithcode.com "trending" "machine learning"`
- `"new paper" "language model" "arXiv"`

Validation notes:

- Confirm title, authors/lab, date, and paper URL.
- Prefer papers with code/data/evaluations, but do not exclude theory or safety work.
- Summarize contribution, method, benchmark/task, and limitation if visible.

## Journalism / news

Prefer outlets with editorial standards, corrections policies, or strong tech desks:

- Reuters, Associated Press, Bloomberg, Financial Times, Wall Street Journal
- The Verge, Wired, MIT Technology Review, Ars Technica, TechCrunch, The Information
- Nature News, Science, IEEE Spectrum
- Official regulator/government pages for policy news
- Official company blogs only for product announcements; label as primary/company source

Search patterns:

- `AI news Reuters OR Bloomberg OR "MIT Technology Review" after:<date>`
- `"artificial intelligence" regulation after:<date>`
- `"OpenAI" OR "Google DeepMind" OR Anthropic AI release after:<date>`

Validation notes:

- Separate reported facts from company claims.
- For controversial or market-moving news, cross-check at least two credible sources when possible.
- Avoid low-quality SEO reposts.

## Trends / social and developer signals

Useful signals:

- GitHub Trending and fast-growing repositories
- Hugging Face models/datasets/spaces trending
- Hacker News front page / high-comment threads
- Reddit communities such as r/MachineLearning, r/LocalLLaMA, r/singularity, r/artificial
- Product release notes and changelogs from major AI tools/frameworks
- X/Twitter only as a weak signal unless tied to primary evidence
- Discord/community posts only if public and attributable
- Google Trends or search interest when available

Search patterns:

- `site:github.com trending AI agents LLM after:<date>`
- `site:huggingface.co/models trending LLM after:<date>`
- `site:news.ycombinator.com AI agents LLM after:<date>`
- `site:reddit.com/r/LocalLLaMA "new model" after:<date>`
- `"release notes" "AI" "LLM" after:<date>`

Validation notes:

- Treat trends as signals, not confirmed facts.
- Prefer measurable evidence: stars, forks, comments, upvotes, downloads, model likes, citations, release frequency.
- Mention the signal source and why it appears to be trending.
